> The photos teamsudda.lk needs, page by page, and how to shoot them. Part of the [project documentation](README.md).

Real photos of real Team Sudda people and jobs are the strongest proof the site can show. Every competitor uses stock images, so this is where we stand apart.

## Rules for every shot

- **Real staff, real sites.** No stock photos or AI-generated images.
- **Full uniform** with the Team Sudda logo visible, and clean, matching equipment.
- **Permission in writing** from the property owner before shooting at a client's home or site. Also get written consent from every staff member who is identifiable.
- **No identifying details.** No house numbers, number plates, client names on documents, or family photos in frame.
- **Natural light** wherever possible. Shoot landscape (3:2) unless a shot below says portrait. Use at least 12 MP, and don't use filters.
- **Before and after** from exactly the same position, height and angle. Mark the spot with tape.

## After the shoot

1. Pick the best 2 or 3 of each shot. Export them as JPG at 2000 px on the long edge, at about 80% quality.
2. Name the files by what they show, for example `team-lobby.jpg` or `kitchen-before-1.jpg`.
3. Put them in a shared folder and send the link. They go into `src/assets/`, and the before and after sets go into `src/lib/constants/gallery.ts`. The gallery page and the homepage preview turn on automatically.
