## Our commitment

Everyone who works on this project, including staff, partners and invited collaborators, deserves a professional, respectful and harassment-free environment, regardless of background, identity or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews and messages.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not share private team discussions, unreleased designs, customer data or credentials outside the team without permission.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help put them right.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or demeaning language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing confidential project material without authorisation.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including this repository and its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

> [!NOTE]
> Report concerns to **[hello@teamsudda.lk](mailto:hello@teamsudda.lk)**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that breach this code, and may restrict or remove access for anyone who does not follow it.
